BEFORE YOU EMPTY YOUR CLOSET

A few loose threads.

New idea. Fair questions.

Can I use my own clothes or a loved one’s belongings?

Choose “Make with my fabric” for your own clothes or a human or pet loved one’s suitable fabric keepsakes. Tell us which buttons, pockets, embroidery or patches matter. A maker must review the item and agree the design and what may be cut with you. Sharing a personal story is optional.

Can I contribute clothes and choose a Rebag-made bag?

Yes. Choose the contribution route to offer eligible clothes and design a bag from Rebag material preferences. The clothes contributed are separate from the materials in your purchased bag. Actual material availability and the final quote must be confirmed.

Which clothes are suitable?

Start with clean, dry, untorn denim, cotton or another sturdy woven fabric. Avoid mould, heavy stains, brittle fabric and very stretchy or delicate materials. Photos help, but a maker must inspect the fabric before confirming suitability and how much is needed.

Where will Rebag launch?

Delhi NCR is our first planned region. Pickup/drop-off locations, charges and exact coverage are still being arranged. Saving a design does not book a collection or reserve a production slot.

What am I paying for?

The planned making fee covers work such as cutting, stitching, lining and standard hardware. Custom work and delivery may cost extra. All prices here are illustrative; a final quote needs your approval before any payment or cutting.

How do clothes turn into reward points?

Each contributed garment gets its own pending record. In the demo it earns 50 points after acceptance, for a later order. Rejected garments earn none; fabric used in your own bag is tracked without contribution points. The illustrative rate is 1 point = ₹1, up to 25% of the making fee. These values are not final commercial terms. Maker reviews here are simulations and balances stay in this browser.

Can I actually order a bag here yet?

This is an interactive concept prototype. You can customize a design, try garment rewards and save or download a sample request on this device. Nothing is sent to a maker, no payment is taken, and no pickup or delivery is booked.
