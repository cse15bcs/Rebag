A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we
begin.

A new idea. A few thoughtful questions.

Can I use a loved one's clothing?

Yes, the own-fabric route includes suitable fabric keepsakes from human loved ones and animal companions. A maker must inspect the item and agree what may be preserved and cut. Sharing a personal story is optional.

Will my contributed clothes become my bag?

In the contribution route, your offered clothes are separate from the Rebag materials used for your bag. If you want your own cloth incorporated, choose the own-fabric route.

When can I use reward points?

Accepted contributions earn demo points for a later order. Pending or rejected garments cannot pay for the current design. The current maker-review controls and wallet are simulations.

Where are you planning to launch?

Our main receiving and dispatch base will be Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. Planned customer cities are Delhi, Meerut, Ghaziabad, Noida and Greater Noida. Exact serviceability and arrangements still need confirmation.

Can I place a paid order today?

This is a concept prototype. You can build a design, save it locally and submit a signed-in design-intent approval. No live payment, maker order or production slot is created. Instagram enquiries are being prepared.
