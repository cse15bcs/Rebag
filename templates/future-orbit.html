BEFORE THE NEXT CHAPTER

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I use a loved one's clothes or a pet's belongings?

Yes, you can propose a loved one's clothing, a companion's bandana, or a textile detail that matters to you. The maker will check whether it can become the main fabric or a preserved panel. You never need to share a personal story.

Will my bag look exactly like the illustration?

The illustration and blueprint describe your design intent. Actual fabric, colour, construction and dimensions need maker confirmation and sample review. We will agree which details to preserve before any cutting.

When can I use clothing reward points?

Only accepted contributions earn demo points. They can be used on a later design, within its redemption limit. Pending and rejected garments earn no spendable points; fabric used for your own bag does not earn contribution rewards.

Can I place an order here today?

You can explore the studio, save a local draft and submit a signed-in design approval. This prototype does not collect real payments or book production. Final pricing, serviceability, fabric acceptance and the making plan must be agreed before an order.

Where are you starting?

Our maker base is Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. We are planning for Delhi, Meerut, Ghaziabad, Noida and Greater Noida. Courier collection and delivery depend on serviceability and a confirmed quote.
