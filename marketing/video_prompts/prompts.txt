1. Human loved one — “Their favourite shirt”

Create a 30-second cinematic preorder film for REBAG, a brand that transforms a loved one’s treasured clothes into a handmade keepsake handbag.

FORMAT
Vertical 9:16, realistic cinematography, natural movement, warm daylight, intimate close-ups, gentle pacing. Premium independent craft documentary style. Colours: warm ivory, pale blue, muted sage and natural wood.

STORY
A young adult is remembering a parent who has passed away. Tell the story through small, familiar objects and gestures. The emotion should feel tender, personal and hopeful.

SCENE 1 — 0–6 seconds
Morning light enters a quiet Indian home. A well-loved pale blue cotton shirt rests over an oak chair. A framed family photograph is softly out of focus nearby. The adult gently touches the shirt’s cuff and its distinctive cream button. Hold on the texture and the hand’s small, natural movement.

SCENE 2 — 6–12 seconds
The adult carefully unfolds the shirt on a table. Their expression shows quiet affection and remembrance. They notice the cream button and smile faintly. Use restrained acting and close framing.

SCENE 3 — 12–20 seconds
Match-cut from the shirt’s fabric to a craftsperson carefully stitching the same pale blue cloth into a handbag. Show the original cream button being preserved as a meaningful detail. Include close-ups of thread, fabric and careful hands.

SCENE 4 — 20–26 seconds
Reveal the completed handbag: pale blue cotton, matching fabric handles, the original cream button and neat visible stitching. The adult holds it, traces the button with a thumb, then carries it into gentle afternoon sunlight.

END CARD — 26–30 seconds
Warm ivory background, deep forest-green lettering:
“REBAG”
“Made from memories. Carried with love.”
“Message us about pre-orders.”

VOICEOVER
Warm, conversational and unhurried:
“That shirt was part of their every day. Now, it holds a thousand little memories. At Rebag, we carefully reimagine treasured clothes as a keepsake bag, preserving the details that matter to you. Something of theirs, to carry into your everyday. Made from memories. Carried with love.”

AUDIO
Quiet original piano, subtle room ambience and delicate stitching sounds. Keep music beneath the voice.

CONTINUITY
Use the same adult, shirt fabric, cream button and finished bag throughout. The final bag must visibly come from the original shirt.

AVOID
Dramatic crying, funerals, hospital scenes, ghosts, supernatural reunions, promises to heal grief, sales pressure, invented testimonials, distorted hands, changing faces, changing fabric patterns or unrelated luxury bags.

If accurate lettering is difficult, leave a clean end card for titles to be added during editing.

2. Animal loved one — “The little bandana”
Create a 30-second cinematic preorder film for REBAG, showing how a beloved pet’s fabric belongings can become a personal keepsake handbag.

FORMAT
Vertical 9:16, realistic cinematography, warm natural light, tactile fabric close-ups, gentle camera movement and understated emotion. Colours: warm ivory, muted terracotta, sage and natural wood.

STORY
An adult remembers their beloved dog through its familiar terracotta cotton bandana. Treat the dog as a cherished family member. The story should feel affectionate, quiet and hopeful.

SCENE 1 — 0–6 seconds
A brief, naturally filmed memory: a golden-brown dog rests its head on its person’s knee beside a sunny window. The dog wears a terracotta bandana with a distinctive cream paw-print pattern. Capture a small affectionate gesture, without exaggerated slow motion.

SCENE 2 — 6–12 seconds
Soft dissolve to the present, in the same room. The adult holds that same bandana. A photograph of the dog sits nearby. Their thumb gently follows one cream paw print. Let the familiar object carry the emotion.

SCENE 3 — 12–20 seconds
A craftsperson carefully incorporates a recognisable section of the bandana into the front panel of a handmade sage cotton handbag. Show thoughtful placement, precise stitches and the preserved paw-print detail. The result should feel personal and practical.

SCENE 4 — 20–26 seconds
The adult lifts the finished bag. Close-up: their hand rests briefly on the bandana patch. They take the bag on an ordinary afternoon walk, with a small, reflective smile. Keep the moment natural.

END CARD — 26–30 seconds
Warm ivory background, deep forest-green lettering:
“REBAG”
“For the companions who became family.”
“Message us about pre-orders.”

VOICEOVER
Warm, intimate and unhurried:
“They made the ordinary days unforgettable. Their favourite spot. Their little bandana. The things that still mean so much. At Rebag, we carefully turn suitable fabric keepsakes into a bag made for you, preserving a familiar detail you can carry every day. For the companions who became family. Carried with love.”

AUDIO
Quiet original piano, soft household ambience and delicate fabric sounds. No sad animal cries or dramatic orchestral swells.

CONTINUITY
Keep the same dog, person, room and exact bandana pattern throughout. The paw-print section on the finished bag must match the original bandana.

AVOID
Animal distress, death scenes, graves, rainbow bridges, ghost animals, replacement-pet messaging, promises to heal grief, pressure to buy, cartoon animals, distorted anatomy or changing fabric patterns.

If accurate lettering is difficult, leave a clean end card for titles to be added during editing.
