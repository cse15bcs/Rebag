## Continuity for every generated clip

The supplied still is the visual authority. Keep the same adult's face, hair and clothing, the warm room, original pale blue fabric and its weave, and the original round cream four-hole button with pale blue thread. In the finished bag, the button remains on the short preserved vertical placket section on the upper centre front. Keep two matching broad blue handles, plain ivory lining, a rectangular body and boxed base. Supporting fabric and lining are separate suitable material; do not suggest a single shirt necessarily supplies every structural part.

Check animated outputs for hand anatomy, button holes, fabric drift, handle count and bag proportions. Image references help continuity but are not exact documentary measurements or fabric tracing. For a physically exact product match, use photographs of the actual garment and sample bag. Regenerate a drifting clip from its original still.

Avoid death scenes, hospitals, funerals, graves, ghosts, candles, distress performances, healing promises, pressure to buy, invented testimonials, urgency, prices, dates, bandanas or pet imagery. Do not introduce scene subtitles or watermarks.
