# Rebag — Made from memories

An English, 30-second, 1080 × 1920 portrait preorder concept film.

## The idea

Someone we love may be gone, while the everyday things they used remain full of meaning. Rebag offers a way to reimagine suitable clothes and fabric keepsakes as a personal handbag. The story includes loved people and animal companions with equal care.

The creative direction is warm, tactile and quiet: cream, sage, dusty blue and terracotta, natural window light, gentle camera movement, generous space, and an original ambient instrumental score. The film uses illustrative AI-generated scenes, rather than customer stories or footage of actual products. It contains no spoken narration.

## Film sequence

| Time | Scene | On-screen message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 seconds | A treasured blue shirt on a chair | Some things hold more than memories. |
| 6–12 seconds | A pet bandana and blanket | For the love that stays with us. |
| 12–18 seconds | A maker carefully stitches a keepsake bag | A new shape. The same story. |
| 18–24 seconds | The finished bag, held gently | Made from memories. Carried with love. |
| 24–30 seconds | Rebag end card | Keep a little of them close. Message us about pre-orders. |

The call to action invites an enquiry; it does not suggest that the current prototype takes payments. No launch date, limited stock, price, delivery date or customer testimonial is invented.

## Making the next version personal

- Use actual sample bags and maker footage when available.
- Preserve meaningful details: a shirt button, an embroidered initial, a small patch, or a fabric tag. Confirm what can safely be incorporated.
- Ask separately for permission to cut any irreplaceable garment, to display a customer's story, and to use their photographs in public marketing.
- Replace the generic message call to action with the real public enquiry or preorder link when it exists.

## Rebuild

From this folder, run `node render.mjs`. It uses FFmpeg and Windows Georgia/Segoe UI fonts. Final assets live in `assets/`; intermediate clips and title files live in `work/`. The image prompts are supplied separately in `image-prompts.json` and were generated using the built-in image-generation tool. The soundtrack is synthesized by the renderer from an original note sequence.
