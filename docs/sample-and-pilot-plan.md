## Why make these samples?

The founder confirmed making one physical Everyday Tote and one Little Mood. Compare the two completed samples before approving the final specifications and product choices. Existing bags can demonstrate workmanship or supply details to discuss with the maker; they are not substitutes for these two selected launch samples.

Concept awareness and unpaid enquiries can continue while samples are prepared. Use representative launch samples before taking paid production commitments. Do not present another design or an illustration as a manufactured sample of these products.

## Brief to discuss with the sample maker

Make one of each launch design using ordinary, replaceable cotton or denim. Do not use irreplaceable keepsakes for the initial construction test. Measurements below come from the prototype and are starting proposals; agree finished measurements, tolerances and construction with the maker.

| Sample | Proposed width x body height x depth | Proposed handle drop | Initial details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday Tote | 32 x 34 x 10 cm | 20 cm | Simple layout, fabric handles and outside pocket, matching the starter concept. |
| Little Mood | 24 x 20 x 8 cm | 14 cm | Small handbag with patchwork layout, matching the starter concept. |

Before making, record the required usable fabric area, reinforcement, lining, closure type, hardware, handle construction, pocket measurements and whether a treasured panel can be preserved without weakening the bag. Record any price difference between own-fabric construction and Rebag-supplied materials.

For each sample, capture:

- Agreed specification and the maker's actual itemized or clearly bundled quote. State what INR 250 includes and excludes.
- Actual finished measurements, fabric quantity, materials used and making time.
- Front, back, side, inside, handle and closure photos.
- Intended everyday load agreed with the maker, the carry test performed, duration and any failures. Record repairs and retest results; do not invent a tested load rating.
- Seam and handle attachment checks, lining/closure operation, loose threads, exposed edges, comfort and preservation-detail placement.
- Changes needed before customer production and the approved master version after corrections.

Once approved, share the same master measurements and construction brief with all five tailors. Check each maker's first bag against that master before scaling their assignments.

## Proposed first pilot

Begin with five orders, subject to sample approval, actual quotes, receiving arrangements and customer communication readiness. This is a proposed trial size, not a founder-confirmed booking target. Where feasible, use one initial order per validated tailor to compare consistency; do not assign a sentimental item to an unvalidated process just to fill an allocation.

1. Receive the Instagram enquiry and record the customer route, design, garment list, service area and preservation needs.
2. Review photos and send a provisional quote as a single total including both courier legs, with an asterisk explaining the inclusion; retain the cost components internally.
3. Agree handover terms and book garment shipping to the Meerut receiving base after its exact address and courier coverage are confirmed. Record tracking and a unique customer/order/garment reference.
4. Photograph receipt, reconcile the item count and inspect suitability. For unsuitable fabric, discuss a revised design or return before cutting; agree return shipping charges.
5. Obtain approval for the final design, fabric placement, what may be cut, remnants handling and final price. The site's design-intent approval alone does not authorize cutting.
6. Confirm payment under the agreed pilot process, assign a validated maker and track production. Record any garment transfer between the Meerut base and the maker. Maker locations are not yet specified. The website payment lab remains a simulation.
7. Check the finished bag against the approved design and preservation instructions. Photograph the result, obtain any further review needed and pack with agreed remnants.
8. Dispatch the checked and packed bag from Meerut, record outbound tracking, confirm delivery and collect private feedback. Public use of a customer's story/photos needs separate permission.

Clothing contributions and own-fabric items remain distinct. Only accepted contributions earn rewards for a later order under agreed commercial rules. The prototype wallet is not a live customer balance; keep a separate controlled record if commercial rewards are introduced in the pilot.

## Courier workflow

The founder confirmed **Meerut, Uttar Pradesh** as the main receiving and dispatch base. Its exact street address and postcode remain to be agreed privately before booking.

Track **customer clothes -> Meerut receiving base** and **checked finished bag from Meerut -> customer** separately. Inspect and label garments on receipt, allocate them to a validated maker, record any internal transfers, and perform final quality checks before dispatch. Returns of unsuitable fabric are a possible additional shipment. Any maker-transfer cost also needs to be captured if applicable.

Planned customer service cities are Delhi, Meerut, Ghaziabad, Noida and Greater Noida. Confirm serviceable postcodes with the selected courier before accepting collection/delivery commitments. This city list does not establish five workshops or five receiving addresses.

Record provider, service coverage, parcel size/weight, booked charge, tracking, sender/receiver, item count, packaging condition and responsibility for delays or loss. Agree customer charges before booking. No provider, rate or coverage is assumed by this plan.

Customer pricing decision: the customer funds collection and delivery through one PIN-based inclusive price. Explain this under products with an asterisk; do not show a separate courier line. Current provider tariffs and the Meerut origin PIN are still pending. See [postcode pricing](./postcode-pricing.md).

## Costing and scale-up

Use [the costing sheet](./pilot-costing.csv). Maker/material cost starts at INR 250; other costs and selling prices stay blank until known. Enter an explicit zero only when a cost genuinely does not apply. Formula totals remain blank until every cost input is numeric.

The prototype's INR 499/399 prices and reward amounts remain illustrative. INR 250 is not the complete delivered cost. Include inbound shipping, outbound shipping, packaging, payment costs if used, reward/discount cost if applicable, rework allowance and other applicable costs before setting a price.

Review all five pilot orders for actual cost, making time, first-pass quality, rework, courier timing, customer approval and satisfaction. Increase accepted orders based on observed results and maker availability; do not open 250 weekly slots solely from the planning multiplication.
