## Product promise

Rebag offers two ways to make use of suitable old clothes: turn the customer's own fabric into a personal bag, or contribute clothes for approved reward points and choose a bag made from Rebag's materials.

Positioning: “Made from memories. Carried with love.”

Serve customers across Delhi NCR, India. The keepsake route includes people remembering human loved ones and animal companions. Customers can also use their own ordinary clothes or contribute clothes without a memorial purpose. A customer can explain the details they want preserved without having to share a personal bereavement story.

## Two product routes

| Route | Customer action | Result | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use my fabric | Choose a shape/design, offer suitable clothes and identify the parts to use or preserve. | A bag made using the customer's own approved fabric, either as the main material or an agreed detail. | The available colour and pattern follow the actual cloth; a colour preview does not promise fabric dyeing or an exact digital match. |
| Clothes for points + a Rebag-made bag | Contribute eligible clothes for inspection, then choose an existing bag or build a bag using Rebag's available materials. | Accepted contributions earn points; the customer selects a Rebag-made bag. | Contributions are tracked separately from the fabric used in the purchased bag. Pending contributions are not spendable points. |

Reward timing is confirmed: points earned from accepted clothing contributions are usable on a later order, not on the order associated with those contributions. Pending or rejected garments do not provide spendable points. Keep this timing in the website copy, customization review and eventual account-linked wallet. The demo's 50 points per garment, ₹1 per point and 25% cap are not final commercial terms.

## Customization window

The founder wants customers to choose the bag's shape and design in a build window. Retain the existing live-preview approach and plan these choices:

1. Choose own fabric or a Rebag-made bag with clothing contributions for points.
2. Choose Everyday Tote or Little Mood as the initial shape.
3. Choose the design/pattern, handles and supported personalization.
4. For own fabric: add fabric photos and notes about placement/preservation; explain that the cloth determines its colour/pattern.
5. For Rebag materials: choose only actual available colour/material options once stock is known.
6. Review the design, approximate price and any contribution/reward status.
7. Start an Instagram conversation; offer WhatsApp when configured. Make clear that opening a chat does not itself submit or confirm an order.

A saved/exported design brief can support the conversation. Do not claim that garment photos or the complete design are automatically transmitted to Instagram/WhatsApp unless that transfer is actually implemented.

## What customers may offer

- Clean, dry, untorn clothing or suitable fabric belongings.
- Begin by evaluating sturdy cotton and denim; a maker must confirm each item.
- A pet bandana or fabric blanket may be incorporated as a panel or accent if suitable; do not assume it can provide the entire bag structure.
- Delicate, damaged, stretchy or unstable material needs individual assessment. Offer a smaller preserved detail or return the item if full construction is unsuitable.
- Non-fabric belongings, such as collars or identification tags, are outside the initial standard offer until a maker confirms a secure attachment method and the customer approves it.

## Preservation agreement

Before accepting or cutting an irreplaceable item, record:

1. The item, its condition and the customer/order reference.
2. The details that must be preserved and the areas that may be cut.
3. The agreed bag design and fabric placement.
4. What happens to unused fabric and any rejected garment.
5. The final quote and the customer's approval.

Never treat a garment as approved for cutting solely because its photo was submitted. Public use of a customer's photographs or story requires a separate permission choice.

Customer pricing decision (revised): show bag prices on the main page and calculate bag/customization charges independently. Collect the PIN and add courier charges as separate lines only in the final checkout/review breakdown. Current provider tariffs and the Meerut origin PIN are still pending. See [postcode pricing](./postcode-pricing.md).
