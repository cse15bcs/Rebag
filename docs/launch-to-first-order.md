The founder wants steps presented in a table to decide what to do first. Priorities below are recommendations, not a selected execution order or authorization to publish, contact customers, spend money or change account access.

## Recommended sequence

Steps 1–5 can progress together. A public enquiry website can precede paid checkout. A first paid pilot order can use a documented manual quote/payment process if its operational requirements are ready; a complete website checkout is a separate milestone and remains in scope below. No fixed launch date or first-order guarantee is implied.
